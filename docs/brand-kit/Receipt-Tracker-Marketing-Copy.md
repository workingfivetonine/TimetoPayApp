## 4. Full feature list (benefit-led)

Drop-in copy for a landing page, app-store description, or pitch — grouped by the benefit each feature sells.

### Scan smarter, type less
- 📸 **AI receipt scanning** — snap a photo and AI instantly pulls out every item, price, and store. No manual entry.
- 📷 **In-app camera** — capture a receipt without leaving TimetoPay to open Photos first.
- 🖼️ **Upload from your gallery** — already have a photo? Import it in a tap.
- 📄 **PDF receipt import** — forward an emailed or digital receipt (up to 10 pages) and let AI do the rest.
- 📤 **Share straight in** — send a receipt to TimetoPay from Photos or Files using your phone's share sheet.
- 🏷️ **Auto-categorized & labeled** — every item gets a category and emoji icon automatically, so your history is organized the moment it's scanned.

### Never overpay again
- 💰 **Automatic price tracking** — watch how prices change over time, per item.
- 🥇 **Best price & best store** — instantly see where each item is cheapest based on your own buying history.
- 🛒 **Smart shopping list** — builds itself from what you actually buy, split into *Regulars* (your staples) and *One-offs*.
- 🛍️ **Shopping Mode** — a dedicated in-store view: your list grouped by store, big checkboxes, a running basket total.
- ⛔ **Ran out, one tap** — mark an item out of stock and it jumps back to the top of your list.
- 🏪 **Store cost-benefit analysis** — factor in delivery fees and minimum-order thresholds to know which store really wins.
- 🌐 **Community price catalog** — see typical prices shoppers near you pay, so you know a good deal when you see one (fully anonymized — we never show whose purchase is whose).

### Stay organized effortlessly
- 📝 **Printable & shareable lists** — export a clean PDF grouped by store, category, or A–Z.
- 🔍 **Search & sort everywhere** — find any item, store, or receipt in seconds across every screen.
- ✏️ **Full control** — edit item names, add notes, fix prices, or swap an item's icon anytime.

### Know where your money goes
- 📊 **Spending analytics** — weekly spend at a glance with automatic HIGH/LOW flags when something's unusual.
- 📈 **Per-item price history** — lowest, average, and highest price for everything you buy.

### A place to talk shop
- 💬 **Community board** — trade tips, deals, and recipes with other shoppers once you've uploaded a couple of receipts. Every post is reviewed before it's visible to anyone else.
- 🚩 **Report and block** — flag anything that shouldn't be there, or stop seeing a person entirely — instant, private, and reversible.

### Private and built for trust
- 🔒 **Your data is yours** — every receipt, store, and price is private to your account by default.
- 🛡️ **Privacy-first community pricing** — aggregated insights without ever exposing an individual's purchases.

### Use it anywhere
- 📱 **Mobile + web** — native iOS and Android apps plus a full web app in any browser, all on the same account.
- 🎉 **Completely free** — every feature above, for every signed-in user. No subscription, no trial, no card required. Ever.

## 5. What signing up gets you

TimetoPay had a paid tier before 2.0 — a 30-day trial, then $5.99/month via Stripe or PayPal. **That's gone.** As of the 2.0 release, every feature is free for every signed-in user, permanently. This section replaces the old subscriber-facing pitch with copy for the same moment (right after sign-up, or on a "what you get" screen) that doesn't promise a bill that never comes.

**Everything, from the moment you sign up:**

- 📸 **Unlimited AI receipt scanning** — snap a photo and AI pulls out every item, price, and store. No typing, no limits, no meter running.
- 📷📄 **Every way to add a receipt** — in-app camera, gallery upload, PDF import, or share-to-app.
- 💰 **Full price history** — track how the prices of everything you buy move over time.
- 🥇 **Best price & best store** — always know where each item is cheapest based on your own shopping.
- 🛒 **Your self-building shopping list** — regulars and one-offs detected automatically, with the lowest price next to each item, plus a dedicated Shopping Mode for the store itself.
- 🏪 **Store cost-benefit analysis** — weigh delivery fees and minimum orders to pick the smartest store.
- 🌐 **Community price catalog & board** — see typical prices other shoppers pay, fully anonymized, and trade tips on the community board.
- 📊 **Spending analytics** — weekly spend trends with automatic high/low flags.
- 📈 **Per-item insights** — lowest, average, and highest price for every item you've bought.
- 📝 **Printable & shareable shopping lists** — clean lists grouped by store, category, or A–Z.
- 🔍 **Search & sort across everything** — find any item, store, or receipt instantly.
- 🔒 **Total privacy** — your receipts and prices stay private to your account, always.
- 📱 **One account, every device** — pick up where you left off on web, iOS, or Android.
- 🆕 **All future updates included** — every new feature we ship, at no extra cost, forever.

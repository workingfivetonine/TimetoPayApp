# TimetoPay — What's New (June 2026)

Thanks for helping test **TimetoPay** at **[5to9shopping.com](https://5to9shopping.com)**! Here's what's new since the last round, what we'd love you to try, and a couple of things still in progress.

> **Before you start:** please open the site in your browser and do a hard refresh (**Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + R**) so you're on the latest version, and sign in.

---

## 🧾 Scanning & receipts
- **Free scanning for everyone.** Free accounts can now scan **one photo at a time** with AI — up to **1 scan/week and 4/month**. The scan screen shows how many you have left. (PDFs and multi-photo uploads remain a paid feature.)
- **Sharper photos = better reading.** Receipt photos now upload at higher quality, so prices and store names read more accurately.
- **Scanning tips** are shown right on the scan screen (lay it flat, good lighting, etc.).
- **Review screen improvements.** After a scan you can fix the store, date, total, and each item before saving — and there's a new **"priced by weight"** toggle for produce/meat (enter price-per-pound × weight and it calculates the line total).

## 🛒 Shopping list
- **Undo.** Marked something "ran out" or removed it by mistake? An **Undo** appears for a few seconds.
- **"Buy More" button.** An item you marked as out now shows **Buy More** to put it right back on the list.
- **Printable list, your way.** When you download the list you can group it **by category, by store, or A–Z**.

## 🏪 Stores
- **Store logos** now appear automatically, with a **Refresh logos** button if any are missing.
- **Website field** on each store, plus tap the address for **Google Maps directions** or look up **hours on Google**.
- The Stores tab loading issue has been fixed.

## 📊 Analytics
- New spending summaries: **"[This month] so far"** and **"Since [the month you joined]"**.
- Spend calendar with a quick **"+"** on any day to add a receipt.

## 💳 Account & subscription
- **Multi-currency display.** Prices now show in your **local currency symbol** based on your country (£, €, ₪, R, etc.). *Note: amounts are shown exactly as entered — nothing is converted.* Set your country under Account.
- After you cancel, your subscription card now clearly says **"Premium access ends [date]"** instead of "renews."
- Heads-up **banners** when a free trial is ending soon or a payment didn't go through.
- **Log Items** entry is simpler: only the item name is required (store and date are optional), and quantity is capped at 1000.
- **Date fields** now open a **calendar picker**.

## 👥 Community board
- The community board and moderation are working again — post tips/deals (available to subscribers with at least 2 uploaded receipts).

## ✉️ Emails
- Welcome, trial-ending, and other emails have a refreshed, consistent look. If you sign up fresh or start/cancel a trial, keep an eye on your inbox (and let us know if anything looks off or doesn't arrive).

---

## 🙏 What we'd love you to test
1. **Scan a few receipts** (photo) — does it read the **store, date, total, and prices** correctly? Try a tricky/crumpled one.
2. **Set your country** (Account) and confirm prices show the **right currency symbol** everywhere.
3. **Shopping list** — mark items "ran out," try **Undo** and **Buy More**, then **download** the list in each grouping.
4. **Stores** — add a store, check the **logo**, **website**, and **directions** links.
5. **Analytics** — do the **this-month / since-join** totals look right?
6. **Add a receipt manually** and via **Log Items** — is anything confusing?
7. On **mobile and desktop** browsers if you can.

## ⚠️ Known / in progress
- Email automations are still being finalized — some emails may not arrive yet during this testing window.
- If you're on **Microsoft Edge** and sign-in feels flaky, try Chrome (Edge's tracking protection can block the login).
- A few advanced items (signup survey, store name auto-complete) are intentionally not in this round.

## 🐞 How to report issues
For anything broken or confusing, please note:
- **What you did** (the steps), **what you expected**, and **what happened**.
- Your **device + browser** (e.g. "iPhone Safari", "Windows Chrome").
- A **screenshot** if you can.

Send it via the in-app **Support** form (Account → Support) or reply to this message. Thank you! 🛒
